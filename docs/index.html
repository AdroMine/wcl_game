Round 1 • Physical Puzzle

THE SHATTERED TABLET

Tomb Gate Alignment • Duration: ~5 Minutes

🏛️ The Story: Walking through the dark expanse of Tartarus, your crew reaches a sealed ancient stone door. You need a codeword to pass. Pieces of a shattered ancient tablet lie on the ground before you.

⚙️ Mechanics

  • 5 Slices on 5 Phones: The tablet is split into 5 vertical slices across your crew's phones (#1 to #5).
  • Physical Phone Order: Players physically place their 5 phones side-by-side in numerical order (#1 to #5) across the table.
  • The Codeword: Carved golden fracture lines align across phone borders to reveal a 2-word codeword (e.g. CRIMSON SHADOW).
  • Team Submission: Any player in the crew can type and submit the answer on their screen.

🏆 Points & Winning Conditions

  • Speed Rewards: Faster team solves earn higher points (Legendary → Swift → Steady).
  • Safety Net: 50 PTS guaranteed floor ensures every crew passes through the tomb gate.
  • Advancement: The round completes when all teams enter the code or the timer concludes.
💡 Facilitator Cue: "Don't just stare at your own phone—stand up, place your 5 phones in order 1 to 5, and read the tablet together!"
Talking points: - Gets everyone active and physically cooperating immediately. - Phones go on the table: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. - The cracks match up like a jigsaw across phone bezels. - Guaranteed floor so no one gets stuck or eliminated.
Round 2 • Prisoner's Dilemma

SHADES IN THE MIST

Prisoner's Dilemma • 3 Clashes • Face-to-Face Negotiation

🏛️ The Story: You escape through the tomb door into a vast cavern hall as thick Stygian mist rolls in. Another crew of shades emerges from the shadows. Are they trustworthy friends or treacherous enemies?

⚙️ Mechanics (Each Clash)

Your crew faces a rival table across 3 escalating clashes:

  1. Private Huddle (50s): Whisper at your table. Decide your true stance: Truce or Ambush?
  2. The Dialogue (40s): Talk with the other team across the room (bluffing is 100% permitted!).
  3. Secret Vote (25s): Every player votes secretly on phone. Majority rules (Negotiator breaks ties).
  4. The Reveal (40s): Both tables' choices unmask simultaneously on screen!

🏆 Points Difference

(Stakes DOUBLE in Clash 2, TRIPLE in Clash 3!)
🤝 Mutual Truce (Cooperation)
Both teams play peaceful → Both win solid, steady points (+75 / +150 / +225 PTS)
🗡️ The Backstab (Betrayal)
Ambush beats Truce → Betrayer steals maximum points (+150 / +300 / +450 PTS); peaceful victim gets 0 PTS!
💥 Mutual Brawl (Conflict)
Both teams attack → Both walk away with scraps (+15 / +30 / +45 PTS)
⚠️ Key Tension: Promises made across the room are non-binding. Will you build trust or cash in on a backstab?
Talking points: - Prisoner's Dilemma adapted for pub teams. - People can talk/bluff with the other team during The Dialogue. - Voting is secret on the phone so nobody knows who voted what until team total locks. - Stakes triple in clash 3, making betrayal very tempting!
Round 3 • Escape Royale

ESCAPE FROM TARTARUS

River Styx Congestion Race • 1,000m to the Exit Portal

🏛️ The Story: Cerberus is howling in the shadows behind you, and the exit portal across the wide River Styx is slowly starting to close. Can your crew reach the portal before it seals forever?

⚙️ Mechanics: The 3 Vessels

🚣 The Rowboat (Safe)

Unlimited capacity • Always advances +150m. Never capsizes.

⛵ The Sailboat (Speed vs Crowds)

Max 30% fleet cap • +300m surge! Capsizes (0m) if overcrowded.

⚡ The Ghost Ship (Solo Gamble)

Exactly 1 crew • +450m leap! Collides (-50m) if 2+ crews board.

🏆 Winning Conditions

  • Starting Head-Start: Total points accumulated from Rounds 1 & 2 convert directly into starting meters.
  • Podium Medals: First 3 crews to cross the 1,000-meter mark win Gold, Silver & Bronze!
  • 🐕‍🦺 Stygian Leash: Tether your rival's boat. If they float: steal 50m. If they crash: drag them down & get +100m! (If your own boat crashes, leash snaps: 0m).
  • Sudden Death: Tie-breaker standoff duel (Shields vs Ram vs Sails) if crews cross simultaneously.
Talking points: - 4 waves of vessel selection with a room-wide shouting/bluffing phase before each wave. - Rowboat is safe. Sailboat is fast but capsizes if crowded. Ghost ship is huge but only 1 crew can take it. - First past 1,000m wins the entire tournament!
