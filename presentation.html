👥 The 5-Player Crews

Every table forms a crew of 5 members with distinct flavor roles:

🧭 Navigator 👁️ Oracle 📖 Scholar 🔥 Torchbearer 🛡️ Negotiator

• Simple & Accessible: Roles are identities and tablet slice markers (no complicated power management).
• Instant Join: 1 player creates the crew; others tap 3 emojis (e.g. 💀 ⚡ 🛡️) on screen. No typing, no camera permissions.

🏆 The Ultimate Objective

  • Rounds 1 & 2: Earn points through teamwork, deduction, and tactical diplomacy.
  • Round 3: Points convert into a starting distance head-start on the River Styx!
  • Victory: First 3 crews to cross the 1,000-meter portal claim Gold, Silver & Bronze.
Talking points: - Fast intro: Welcome to Underworld. - 5 people per crew. Everyone gets their own phone, no app download needed. - Joining is as easy as tapping 3 emojis shown on the crew leader's phone. - 3 rounds, ~25 minutes total. Each round tests a completely different skill.
Round 1 • Physical Puzzle

THE SHATTERED TABLET

Physical Screen Alignment • ~5 Minutes

🧩 The Story & Mechanic

  • The Setup: You reach a sealed ancient tomb door in Tartarus. A cracked stone tablet lies on the ground.
  • 5 Phone Slices: The tablet is split into 5 vertical slices across your crew's 5 phones (#1 to #5).
  • Physical Cooperation: Players must place their 5 phones side-by-side in numerical order across the pub table.
  • The Discovery: Golden fracture lines connect across screens to reveal a hidden 2-word ancient codeword.

⏱️ How It Works & Scores

  • Speed Wins: Faster solves yield higher points (Legendary $\rightarrow$ Swift $\rightarrow$ Steady).
  • Any Teammate Can Submit: Once you read the codeword, type it in on any phone in the crew.
  • Stuck? Progressive Hints: Hints unlock at 2m, 3m, and 4m revealing who holds the left, right, and center pieces (small -30 PTS deduction each).
  • Safety Net: 50 PTS guaranteed floor—everyone gets through the door!

💡 Key Takeaway for Players: "Don't just stare at your own screen—physically stand up, arrange your phones on the table in order 1 through 5, and read the tablet together!"

Talking points: - This gets people off their individual screens and physically cooperating immediately. - Phones go on the table: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. - The cracks match up like a jigsaw puzzle across borders. - Hints exist so nobody gets stuck forever.
Round 2 • Social Dilemma

SHADES IN THE MIST

3 Clashes • Face-to-Face Negotiation • Bluffing Allowed

⚔️ How Each Clash Runs

Your crew is paired against a rival table for 3 escalating clashes:

  1. Private Huddle (50s): Whisper at your table. Truce or Ambush?
  2. The Dialogue (40s): Turn to the rival table, look them in the eye, and negotiate. (Bluffing is 100% permitted!).
  3. Secret Vote (25s): Every player votes privately on their phone (Majority rules; 🛡️ Negotiator breaks ties).
  4. The Reveal (40s): Both tables' choices unmask simultaneously!

⚖️ The Stances & Stakes (Triples in Clash 3!)

  • 🤝 Mutual Truce: Both crews play peaceful $\rightarrow$ Both win solid points.
  • 🗡️ The Backstab (Ambush vs Truce): The betrayer steals maximum points; the peaceful victim gets 0!
  • 💥 Mutual Brawl (Ambush vs Ambush): Both try to backstab $\rightarrow$ Both walk away with scraps.
🕊️ Moral Legacy

• Honourable (3 Truces): +75m head-start boost in Round 3!
• Betrayed: Receives a Cerberus revenge token for Round 3!

💡 Key Takeaway for Players: "Promises made across the room are not binding. Will you build trust for guaranteed points, or cash in on a brutal backstab?"

Talking points: - Classic Prisoner's Dilemma, but team vs team in a loud pub. - People can lie to each other's faces during The Dialogue. - Voting is secret on the phone so nobody knows who voted what until the team total is locked. - Stakes triple in clash 3, making betrayal very tempting!
Round 3 • The Climax

ESCAPE FROM TARTARUS

River Styx Congestion Royale • 1,000m Race to the Portal

Safe & Steady

🚣 The Rowboat

Capacity: Unlimited

Outcome: +150m always.

Never capsizes. Reliable way to tick forward while rivals greedily crash.

Speed vs Crowds

⛵ The Sailboat

Capacity: Max 30% of fleet

Success: +300m surge!

Overcrowded: CAPSIZES into the river (0m)!

Solo High-Stakes

⚡ Ghost Ship

Capacity: Exactly 1 crew

Solo Surge: +450m leap!

2+ Crews: Crash! (-50m penalty)!

🏁 Starting Head-Start & Deciding Winners

• Total points from R1 & R2 convert into your starting river meters.
• 🕊️ Winds of Olympus: +75m bonus if you never betrayed anyone in R2!
• Victory: First 3 crews across 1,000m win Gold, Silver & Bronze. (Sudden Death duel if tied!).

🐕 Cerberus Revenge Attack

• Teams betrayed in Round 2 hold a Cerberus Honey-Cake Treat.
• Guess which vessel your backstabber picks: if right, Cerberus attacks them (-150m drag back) and gives you a +150m leap!

Talking points: - Final battle: 4 waves of vessel picking. - You shout and try to trick other tables into the wrong boat during the 55s bluff phase. - Rowboat is safe. Sailboat is fast but capsizes if greedy. Ghost ship is huge but only works if you're the ONLY crew on it. - First past 1,000m wins the entire tournament!
