👥 The 5-Player Crews

Every table forms a 5-player crew with unique flavor roles:

🧭 Navigator 👁️ Oracle 📖 Scholar 🔥 Torchbearer 🛡️ Negotiator
  • Zero Friction: Roles identify members and tablet puzzle slots; no complex powers to memorize.
  • Instant Join: 1 player creates team; others tap a 3-emoji code (e.g. 💀 ⚡ 🛡️). No typing or cameras.

🏆 The Ultimate Objective

  • Rounds 1 & 2 (Points Pool): Earn points through physical teamwork, visual puzzle-solving, and negotiation.
  • Round 3 (Head-Start): Points directly convert into a starting distance advantage on the River Styx!
  • Victory: First 3 crews to cross the 1,000-meter exit portal win Gold, Silver & Bronze medals.
Talking points: - Quick welcome. Event runtime is ~25 minutes across 3 distinct rounds. - 5 players per crew. Joining is as simple as tapping 3 emojis shown on the captain's phone. - Roles are just identities and physical tablet slots. - Points from R1 & R2 convert into a head-start in R3.
Round 1 • Physical Cooperation Puzzle

THE SHATTERED TABLET

Tomb Gate Alignment • Duration: ~5 Minutes

🧩 The Story & Mechanic

  • The Premise: An ancient tomb gate blocks Tartarus. A cracked stone tablet lies on the ground.
  • 5 Slices on 5 Phones: The tablet is split into 5 vertical slices distributed across the crew's phones (#1 to #5).
  • Physical Alignment: Players must place their 5 phones side-by-side in numerical order across the pub table.
  • The Codeword: Carved fracture lines align across screen borders to reveal a 2-word codeword (e.g., CRIMSON SHADOW).

⏱️ Points & Progressive Hints

  • Speed Rewards: Faster team solves earn maximum points (Legendary $\rightarrow$ Swift $\rightarrow$ Steady).
  • Anyone Can Submit: Any teammate can type and submit the answer on their screen.
  • Progressive Hints: Automatic hints unlock at 2m, 3m, and 4m pinpointing left, right, and center pieces (-30 PTS penalty).
  • Safety Floor: 50 PTS guaranteed floor ensures every crew makes it through the gate.
💡 Key Facilitator Prompt: "Don't sit and stare at your own phone—stand up, line up your 5 phones in order 1 through 5 on the table, and read the tablet together!"
Talking points: - Gets everyone active and physically cooperating immediately. - Phones go on the table: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. - The cracks match up like a jigsaw across phone bezels. - Guaranteed floor so no one gets stuck or eliminated.
Round 2 • Social Dilemma & Bluffing

SHADES IN THE MIST

3 Clashes vs Rival Table • Face-to-Face Negotiation • Bluffing Allowed

⚔️ How Each Clash Runs

Your crew faces a rival table across 3 escalating clashes:

  1. Private Huddle (50s): Whisper at your table. Truce or Ambush?
  2. The Dialogue (40s): Turn to rival table, look them in the eyes, and negotiate. (Bluffing is 100% allowed!).
  3. Secret Vote (25s): Every player votes secretly on phone. Majority rules (Negotiator breaks ties).
  4. The Reveal (40s): Stances unmask simultaneously on screen!

⚖️ Stances & Long-Term Legacy

  • 🤝 Mutual Truce: Both play peaceful $\rightarrow$ Both win solid, safe points.
  • 🗡️ The Backstab: Ambush beats Truce $\rightarrow$ Betrayer steals huge points; victim gets 0!
  • 💥 Mutual Ambush: Both attack $\rightarrow$ Both get scraps.
  • 📈 Escalating Stakes: Points DOUBLE in Clash 2, TRIPLE in Clash 3!
🕊️ Legacy Bonus for R3: Unbroken peace grants a +75m head-start in R3; betrayed teams get a Cerberus revenge token!
💡 Key Dynamic: "Promises made across the room are not binding. Will you build trust for reliable points, or cash in on a brutal betrayal?"
Talking points: - Prisoner's Dilemma adapted for pub teams. - People can lie to each other's faces during The Dialogue. - Voting is secret on the phone so nobody knows who voted what until team total locks. - Stakes triple in clash 3, making betrayal very tempting!

ESCAPE FROM TARTARUS

River Styx Congestion Race • 1,000m to the Exit Portal

Safe & Steady

Speed vs Crowds
⛵ Sailboat

Solo High-Stakes
⚡ Ghost Ship

Capacity: Exactly 1 crew

Advance: +450m leap!

🏁 Deciding the Winners

  • Head-Start: Points from R1 & R2 convert into starting river meters (+75m honor bonus).
  • Podium Finish: First 3 crews across 1,000m win Gold, Silver & Bronze. (Sudden Death standoff if tied!).

🐕 Cerberus Revenge Attack

  • Crews betrayed in Round 2 can throw a treat to Cerberus.
  • Guess your nemesis's boat: if correct, they are dragged back -150m and you surge +150m!
Talking points: - 4 waves of vessel selection with a room-wide shouting/bluffing phase before each wave. - Rowboat is safe. Sailboat is fast but capsizes if crowded. Ghost ship is huge but only 1 crew can take it. - First past 1,000m wins the entire tournament!
